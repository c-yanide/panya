How we handle your data

Last updated November 2026

Panya is an early prototype from a Stanford Learning Design & Technology capstone. When you join the waitlist, here’s what happens to the information you share.

What we collect

Your email address, the optional answer to “What best describes you?”, and which sign-up form you used. Nothing else.

Why we collect it

To invite you to try Panya when a spot opens, and to understand who is interested so we can design it well. We won’t sell your information or use it for advertising.

Research

Early users may be invited to share feedback as part of the capstone. Joining the waitlist doesn’t sign you up for any study. We’ll always ask for your consent first, and you can say no.

Who else handles it

Sign-ups are collected through Formspree, a form service that stores submissions on our behalf. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long we keep it

Until the project ends or you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email we send, or email us at cyada@stanford.edu to see, change, or delete your information.
